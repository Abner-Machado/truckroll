=== OK modelo: openrouter/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017 | 27.3s ===
Budget the telemetry log against the flash's rated program/erase endurance and store it in a wear-leveled, RAM-buffered circular buffer instead of writing every sample continuously to a fixed set of flash sectors. That spreads erase cycles across the whole array and cuts the total written, keeping cumulative P/E cycles far below the endurance limit for the intended service life, so the memory still retains data past year four instead of exhausting a small region and latching the unit into an unrecoverable state.
