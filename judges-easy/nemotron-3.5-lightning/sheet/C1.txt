RULE: 7

Use a mutex with priority inheritance to guard the shared resource instead of a binary semaphore. Priority inheritance causes the low-rate housekeeping task to temporarily inherit the high-rate task's priority, allowing it to complete its critical section and release the lock promptly, preventing the high-rate task from missing its deadline due to unbounded priority inversion.
