The engineering decision to not calculate or enforce a write budget based on the flash memory's 3000 P/E cycle endurance rating, allowing unlimited writes that eventually exhausted per-cell limits.

Implementing a write budget would have capped total writes at a level sustainable over the device's four-year lifetime, preventing any cell from reaching its endurance threshold.
