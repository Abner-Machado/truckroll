The single engineering decision that would most likely have prevented this is implementing hardware revision detection with version gating in the firmware update process, so that revision A units would refuse to apply a revision B-specific update. This would have prevented it because the update would have been blocked on revision A hardware before it could brick the devices, avoiding the need for physical recovery.
